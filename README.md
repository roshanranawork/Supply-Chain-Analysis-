Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

Driving Efficiency. Delivering Value.

A 3-page Power BI dashboard that gives a complete view of procurement, supplier, and logistics performance (Jan 2025 to Jan 2026), built to support smarter supply chain decisions.

Dashboard Cover

Business Problem

Procurement spend came in about ₹18M below target, but only 63.71% of orders were delivered on time. This dashboard shows where spend deviates from target, which suppliers carry risk, and which transport modes and carriers cause delivery delays.

Key Metrics
Metric	Value
Actual Procurement Spend	₹991.94M
Total Purchase Orders	480
On-Time Delivery	63.71%
Average Lead Time	10.43 days
Rejection Rate	0.82%
Key Insights
Delivery is the main problem, not cost. Spend is under target, but on-time delivery is only 63.71%.
Road shipments dominate delays (302), followed by Rail (113) and Air (72). Carrier SLAs are the place to focus.
Rail delays are concentrated in one carrier: RailCargo Connect (49), then TCI Express (37) and SwiftHaul Logistics (27).
Quality is strong. The rejection rate is only 0.82%.
Supplier base is stable: 73% of suppliers are Medium risk and 27% Low risk.
Largest spend variance: Office & IT Supplies (₹6.3M), then Electrical & Electronics (₹3.9M).
Order status: 93.57% closed, 5.26% cancelled, 1.17% open.
Lead time (10.43 days) leaves room for further optimization.

Features

Filters for month, supplier, and date range, plus quarter buttons
Recommendations
Renegotiate SLAs with the carriers behind most delays, starting with RailCargo Connect.
Shift or re-tender Road shipments on the worst-performing routes and regions.
Review Office & IT Supplies and Electrical & Electronics pricing to cut spend variance.
